📊 DATASETS (DOWNLOAD & USE THESE)

You MUST use these datasets and explain how to integrate them:

1. BigBasket Products Dataset (MASTER DATASET)

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/surajjha101/bigbasket-entire-product-list-28k-datapoints

Indian grocery products
Contains: product_name, brand, category, price
This will act as the MASTER PRODUCT TABLE
2. Blinkit / Grofers Dataset (Price + Availability)

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tomtillo/blinkit-grocery-list-price-city-date

Indian quick-commerce data
Adds pricing variation and product availability
3. Groceries Dataset (Market Basket)

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/heeraldedhia/groceries-dataset

Transaction dataset (~38k rows)
Used for consumption patterns and recommendation
4. Grocery Inventory & Sales Dataset

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/salahuddinahmedshuvo/grocery-inventory-and-sales-dataset

Contains stock, quantity, expiry, sales
5. Smart Waste Inventory Dataset

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/wildlearner/smart-waste-inventory-dataset

Contains spoilage probability and waste labels
6. Fruit & Vegetable Prices Dataset (India)

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kirbysasuke/fruit-and-vegetable-prices

Indian pricing trends (mandi-level data)
7. Perishable Goods Management Dataset (Optional Advanced)

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/likithagedipudi/perishable-goods-management
